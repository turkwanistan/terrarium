Moss character pass — identity before detail

The first Godot pilot accidentally invented a different dog: longer/lower body, oversized horizontal head/ear mass, collar, and idle bob. This pass re-bases Godot on the accepted Canvas Moss silhouette, then adds restrained Godot-specific detail: warmer multi-value fur clusters, eye catchlight/brow, brighter cream blaze/muzzle/chest, inner-ear separation, paw accents, and a warmer tail tip. Idle is planted again; walk and inspect use the accepted authored key-pose vocabulary.
